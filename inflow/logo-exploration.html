Strongest two directions

Recommendation 1

C02 — Lemniscate

A continuous figure-eight with no start or end. It captures the flowing, ribbon-like quality of the reference images without copying them. The crossing point is the icon's centre of gravity — calm, balanced, slightly mathematical.

  • No beginning, no end — suggests sustained, unhurried focus
  • Geometric but not rigid — the bezier curves give it life
  • Highly distinctive — nothing else in the app icon landscape looks like this
  • Works monochrome at every size; stroke weight scales cleanly
  • No symbolic baggage (not a checkmark, not a bolt, not a brain)
Recommendation 2

C03 — Saturn

A circle with a tilted orbital ring. Directly captures the planetary reference image while translating it to pure geometry. The faint back arc creates genuine depth — the ring goes around the planet, not through it.

  • Immediate depth and dimensionality without gradients or blur
  • The orbit metaphor fits inFlow naturally — contained motion, regular rhythm
  • Two elements (planet + ring) create enough visual complexity without noise
  • Distinctive and memorable at dock/tab icon scale
  • Slightly warmer than a pure ring — the planet gives it a centre of gravity
