Why it exists

Built for the days when
starting is the hard part.

Some days you do not need a bigger task system. You need a quiet place to land, choose the next step, and begin.

What it helps with

Four things inFlow does well.

Choose one focus

Write down what matters most today. Not the list. One thing. Then write the next small step.

Start with 5 minutes

When even 25 minutes feels like too much, start with 5. The timer is there to help you begin, not to track you.

Park distracting thoughts

Note the things pulling your attention. Get them out of your head so you can come back to them later.

Find your place again

After a meeting, a break, or an interruption. Open inFlow, see your focus, and remember where you left off.

Install

Install inFlow on your computer.

inFlow runs in your browser, but you can install it so it opens like a small desktop app.

Mac

  1. Open inFlow in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click Install.
  4. Pin inFlow to your Dock if you want quick access.

Windows

  1. Open inFlow in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click Install.
  4. Pin inFlow to your taskbar or Start menu.

Safari support can vary. Chrome or Edge gives the most reliable install experience.

Privacy

Private by default.

Your focus board stays on your device. inFlow does not need an account and does not track what you write.
