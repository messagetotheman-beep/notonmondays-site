How we help

We embed alongside founders and leadership teams as operating partners. This is what that looks like in practice.

Not On Mondays works with organisations that need structured senior support: not a consultant who produces a report and leaves, but someone who takes responsibility for outcomes and builds capability that stays.

The work tends to fall into four areas. They are not rigid packages, and most engagements draw on more than one. But they are clear enough to describe the kinds of problems we are built to solve.

01

Operating Layer

For organisations that need structure, rhythm and senior support across delivery, operations and digital decision-making. When the gap between strategy and execution is too wide, this is where we close it.

Website Clarity

For businesses whose website no longer reflects the quality of the work, the offer, or the client experience. We fix the clarity problem first, then build from there.

Learn more → 03

Search & Visibility

For organisations that need to be found by the people already looking for help. Across Google, AI-assisted search and useful content that earns trust before anyone picks up the phone.

Delivery & Operations

For teams that are busy, stretched or context-switching, and need clearer ways of moving work forward. We bring structure, pace and senior judgment to delivery.
