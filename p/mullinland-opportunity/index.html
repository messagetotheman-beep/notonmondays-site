Initial observations

What we noticed about Mullinland

Prepared from initial research into the Mullinland business, its positioning, and its market context. These are observations, not criticisms.

01

Strong existing credibility

Trust in this sector is earned slowly. The foundations at Mullinland are clearly there. The question is whether that credibility is visible and transferable before a direct conversation needs to happen.

02

Positioning that could be sharper

Strategic land advisory sits at a specific intersection: landowner representation, planning strategy, development opportunity. The work deserves a digital presence that communicates this with the same precision.

03

A trust gap before first contact

Landowners do their own research before anyone picks up the phone. For a firm of this calibre, the digital experience does not yet fully support that decision. The gap is worth closing.

04

Operational leverage opportunity

When a founder is the primary source of expertise, capacity is always the constraint. The opportunity is to use digital experience to qualify, educate, and build confidence in prospective clients, before Chris needs to be directly involved.

A familiar pattern

What often happens in founder-led specialist advisory businesses

This is a well-recognised pattern in high-quality professional services. It is not a failure. It is the natural shape of a business built by someone who is genuinely good at the work.

01

The founder's expertise becomes the business. Enquiries, qualification, education, and trust-building all flow through one person. This works, until capacity becomes the constraint.

02

The same explanations get repeated. What strategic land advice involves, what the process looks like, what landowners can expect. Again and again, in early-stage enquiries.

03

Qualification depends on direct conversation. Time goes to enquiries that are not yet ready or not well matched. That time is hard to recover.

04

Marketing feels like more work. Structured content sits outside the delivery rhythm and rarely gets prioritised. Rational, but it compounds.

05

Growth depends on the founder's availability. Referrals work well, but scaling beyond them requires infrastructure: positioning, clarity, and systems that work independently of any individual's calendar.

Opportunity areas

Where the clearest opportunities sit

Six areas where a clearer digital experience could meaningfully improve how Mullinland attracts, qualifies, and works with the right clients.

01 · Positioning

Sharper positioning in a specialist market

These disciplines are often grouped together in how firms present themselves. A clearer statement of what Mullinland specifically does, and for whom, would help the right landowners self-identify and reach out with confidence.

Commercial opportunity: fewer misaligned enquiries. Better-matched clients from the outset.

02 · Conversion

Clearer pathways from interest to conversation

A landowner who finds Mullinland online is already considering their options. Clearer signposting, a straightforward first question, and a low-friction way to start a conversation would all improve conversion.

Commercial opportunity: more enquiries from people who are already half-decided.

03 · Trust architecture

Building trust before the first call

Trust is built through evidence: outcomes, process transparency, and the sense that a firm genuinely understands a landowner's situation. Much of this work currently depends on direct interaction. A well-structured digital presence can do some of it independently.

Commercial opportunity: warmer, better-prepared enquiries. Less time spent building basic confidence.

04 · Authority content

Educational content as a quiet trust signal

Landowners often have significant knowledge gaps. Well-written content covering planning timescales, development potential, and what a strategic land agreement involves demonstrates expertise while reducing the burden of early-stage education in every direct conversation.

Commercial opportunity: credibility and authority that do not require Chris to be in the room.

05 · Landowner entry point

A clear starting point for curious landowners

Many landowners do not know if their land has development potential or what to expect. A simple, reassuring entry point framed for a landowner not a planning professional, such as "Does your land have development potential?", removes friction at the earliest stage.

Commercial opportunity: reach landowners who are curious but uncertain, before they go elsewhere.

06 · Enquiry quality

Better-informed enquiries from the start

The quality of an initial enquiry is determined by how well someone understood what they were approaching. A clearer digital experience sets expectations and means every conversation starts from a more informed baseline.

Commercial opportunity: time saved on qualification. Conversations that go further, faster.

Operational leverage

Reducing the work that depends
on Chris directly

The goal is not more leads. It is better conversations with better-informed people, with less of the repetitive groundwork that currently happens before any real advisory work begins.

"There may be opportunities to reduce the amount of explanation, qualification, and trust-building that currently depends on Chris directly."

01

Clearer qualification pathways. A well-structured set of entry questions, covering land ownership, situation, and goals, helps prospective clients self-assess before making contact. This filters enquiries without any active effort from Chris.

02

Educational guidance that works independently. Content that explains planning timescales, what strategic land involves, and what landowners can realistically expect. Available at any time, not dependent on a conversation to deliver it.

03

Expectation setting before first contact. A clear description of the process, typical timescales, and how Mullinland works means the first conversation starts at a more productive point. Both sides get more from it.

04

Smarter enquiry capture. A structured enquiry format, with the right questions in the right order, saves time on both sides. Signals professionalism. Means responses can be more targeted from the outset.

05

Strategic FAQs. A small set of carefully written questions and answers addressing the most common early-stage concerns. Reduces the same conversation happening repeatedly. Demonstrates expertise without any ongoing effort to deliver it.

06

Visual process explanation. A clear, honest illustration of the process, from initial enquiry to appointment to advisory relationship, builds confidence in the firm without requiring a direct conversation to do so.

Client journey

How digital experience supports the advisory relationship

Each step in the journey from initial uncertainty to a long-term advisory relationship is an opportunity for digital experience to reduce friction and reduce the demand on direct founder time.

01

Landowner uncertainty

Awareness and early research

A landowner becomes aware their land may have development potential and begins researching who can help them understand their options.

Digital role: clear positioning · findability · first impression

02

Clear explanation

Understanding what is involved

The landowner needs to understand what strategic land advice means and what to realistically expect, before they are ready to have a direct conversation.

Digital role: educational content · process clarity · FAQs

03

Strategic guidance

Assessing their own situation

The landowner assesses whether their land has development potential and whether Mullinland is the right adviser. At this stage they are effectively qualifying themselves.

Digital role: landowner entry point · qualification framework

04

Trust building

Developing confidence in Mullinland

Before making contact, the landowner looks for evidence of expertise, track record, and approach. A well-structured digital presence provides this without a direct conversation.

Digital role: outcomes · expertise signals · process transparency

05

First conversation

A more productive initial consultation

The landowner makes contact. Because expectations have already been set, the conversation is immediately more productive. Less time on explanation. More time on the advisory work that only Chris can do.

Digital role: structured enquiry capture · expectation setting

06

Long-term relationship

Advisory appointment and ongoing work

The landowner appoints Mullinland. Because the earlier stages were well-supported, the relationship starts from a stronger foundation: better aligned, better informed, with clearer mutual expectations.

Digital role: ongoing credibility · referral infrastructure

Recommendations

Practical areas worth addressing

Calm, prioritised suggestions. None of them require large-scale change. Each one addresses a specific and addressable opportunity.

01

Positioning refinement. A clearer, more specific statement of what Mullinland does, for whom, and what makes it the right choice, written for a landowner, not a planning professional.

02

Homepage restructure. Rebuild the homepage around the landowner's decision journey: from initial uncertainty, through explanation, to a clear and low-friction first step.

03

Trust architecture. Outcomes, process, and approach communicated with enough specificity to be credible, without depending on a direct conversation to do this work.

04

Landowner entry point. A clear, reassuring starting question that opens a structured pathway for landowners who are curious about their land's potential but not yet ready to make contact.

05

Educational authority. A small set of clear, expert explanations of what strategic land involves, written for landowners, not professionals. Reduces early-stage qualification burden significantly.

06

Mobile experience. A clean, legible, fast-loading mobile experience is not optional. Landowners research on mobile. The current experience does not serve this as well as it could.

07

Enquiry qualification. A structured initial enquiry format that collects the right information (land type, location, situation, goals) so every first conversation starts from a more useful and productive baseline.

08

Operational leverage. Strategic FAQs, process visualisation, and expectation-setting content that reduce the amount of repetitive groundwork in early-stage client conversations.
