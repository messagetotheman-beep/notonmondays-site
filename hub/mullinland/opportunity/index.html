Opportunity note

A note on Mullinland's digital opportunity

Mullinland has the credibility and the track record in strategic land advisory. The gap is in how that is expressed online. This note sets out what we see and what we think is worth doing first.

See the opportunity
What we see

Four things worth noting

The positioning is buried.

Why it matters

Mullinland's expertise in strategic land promotion and development advisory is not clearly expressed online. Landowners and development partners who could benefit most cannot quickly tell whether Mullinland is the right advisory for their land.

The enquiry path is unclear.

Why it matters

For many landowners, strategic land decisions are unfamiliar and long-term. Before reaching out, most will need to understand the process. Without a clear signal about how Mullinland works, that conversation may never start.

Positioning is happening in every call.

Why it matters

Without a clear online presence, Mullinland is likely doing significant positioning work in every early conversation. Good digital materials should do that work before the call starts.

The track record is not visible.

Why it matters

The track record is strong, but it is not surfaced in a way that speaks to the landowners and development partners Mullinland wants to work with. Credibility in strategic land requires evidence. Evidence needs to be findable.

The opportunity

Three areas worth addressing

Clearer positioning

What Mullinland does, for what kinds of land, and at what stage of the planning and promotion process. Stated plainly and visible immediately. The scope of the advisory relationship, the planning context, the expected outcome. No ambiguity about fit.

Better qualified enquiries

A homepage that signals clearly to the right kind of landowner or development partner, so the first conversation starts from a position of shared understanding. Less time on introductions, more time on the land.

Less repeated explanation

Digital materials that explain the advisory proposition clearly, before the first call. So Mullinland's time goes to the strategic land work that matters, not to re-explaining the basics of site promotion and planning process in every early conversation.
