Client workspace · Search and visibility

How SRB Gets Found

Helping the right people find SRB at the moment they need legal support.

Last updated: 6 May 2026
← Dashboard

Overview

Search and visibility is not an add-on. It is part of how the site keeps working. The goal is not to optimise for rankings in the abstract. It is to make sure that people already looking for legal help can find SRB, understand what they offer, and know what to do next.

How we approach this

  • 01
    Understanding demand We identify what people are already searching for: the questions they ask, the situations they are in, and the services they are trying to understand. This shapes what the site needs to say and where it needs to say it.
  • 02
    Creating useful content We improve key service pages and create clear, practical guidance that answers real client questions. Content that is useful to a potential client is also content that search engines and AI tools understand and rank.
  • 03
    Structuring visibility We make the site easier for Google and other tools to read and index correctly. This includes technical structure, page organisation, and how services and lawyers are described. It covers both traditional search and AI-assisted search, without requiring separate work for each.

How visibility actually improves

  • It takes time Visibility improves gradually as content is published, indexed, and trusted. There is no shortcut to this. What matters is that the work is continuous and that each piece of content is genuinely useful.
  • Content should answer real questions Pages written to answer the questions actual clients have outperform pages written to target keywords. The distinction matters in practice: one requires understanding what a client is going through, the other does not.
  • Easier to find and easier to use Search performance and site quality are connected. A site that is easier to navigate, clearer in its language, and faster to load tends to rank better and convert better. Improving one improves the other.

Why this is not a separate workstream

Search and visibility is built into how we improve the site, not added on top. When we improve a service page, we improve its clarity, its structure, and its findability at the same time. The same work that helps a potential client understand what SRB offers also helps a search engine understand it. There is no separate SEO project running alongside the main work.
