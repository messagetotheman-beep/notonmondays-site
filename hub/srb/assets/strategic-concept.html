Making it easy to call
The phone number sits permanently in the top-right corner of every page. For many of SRB's clients — particularly older clients or those in the middle of something stressful — calling is the most natural first step. The current website makes people hunt for that number. Every visit that ends in a phone call is a new instruction. The navigation should never be what stands in the way of that.

The headline: speaking to how clients actually feel
Most people arrive at a solicitor's website feeling anxious. They don't know what they need, they're worried about cost, and they're afraid of saying the wrong thing. "Legal advice that actually makes sense" addresses that directly — positioning SRB as the firm that won't make a client feel like an outsider in their own legal matter. That is a genuine differentiator in this market, and it reflects exactly what your 40-year reputation is built on. It also earns its place in Google search: the plain English phrasing matches how real people in Twickenham and Teddington actually search for a solicitor.

Written for people, not lawyers
Nobody types "residential conveyancing" into Google. They type "help buying a house solicitor Twickenham." The service names here match the language clients actually use. Each card links to its own dedicated page, which Google indexes individually — meaning SRB can appear in search results for eight distinct practice areas, not just once for the firm name. A client who arrives looking for family law sees a clear path straight to that service, rather than reading through everything the firm offers to find what they need. Clarity at this point is the difference between an enquiry and a bounce.

Your people: the firm's most underused asset online
SRB's reputation rests on personal relationships. Yet most law firm websites hide their team. This design puts your solicitors on the homepage — named, with their specialism visible. Each person links through to their own profile page, which Google indexes as a named professional in your local area. When someone searches "family law solicitor Teddington," Lisa Broddle's profile page can appear directly in results. This is one of the most effective and underused local search strategies available to a firm of SRB's size — and it costs nothing beyond good content.

Reviews: your reputation made visible and searchable
SRB has decades of satisfied clients. The current website does very little to surface that. Properly structured review content signals to Google that SRB is a trusted local business — directly influencing where you appear in search results. Notice that each testimonial here names both the service and the location. That is intentional: "Family Law, Teddington" is exactly what someone nearby might search. Reviews are not just reassurance for the visitor — they are search content. Every review that names a service and a location is doing quiet SEO work on your behalf.

Guides: the most cost-effective way to win new clients online
When someone faces a legal situation for the first time, they search for answers before they search for a firm. A guide titled "What actually happens during a house purchase?" appears in Google when someone types exactly that question — and brings them to SRB before they have spoken to anyone else. Your solicitors already know this material. The opportunity is simply to put it online in language the public can find and understand. The firm that answers the question first earns the trust that converts to an instruction. This is a long-term asset: a well-written guide works for years without additional cost.

Catching people before they leave
A significant proportion of website visitors leave without taking any action — not because they are not interested, but because they are not sure what to do next. This panel is designed to catch that moment. "Not sure where to start?" validates the feeling rather than ignoring it, and offers two low-commitment next steps: call, or find us. The language deliberately avoids anything formal — no "submit an enquiry," no "book a consultation." For a firm whose reputation rests on personal service, the language here should feel indistinguishable from how your solicitors actually speak to clients on the phone.

Local presence: the detail that Google rewards
Having both office addresses consistently displayed — with the correct firm name, full address, and phone number on every page — is one of the most important factors in local search ranking. Google uses this to decide which firms appear in the map results when someone nearby searches for a solicitor. Any inconsistency across your website, your Google Business Profile, and third-party directories reduces confidence and suppresses your ranking. This is a straightforward fix with a meaningful impact on how often SRB appears when local clients are actively looking for legal help.
