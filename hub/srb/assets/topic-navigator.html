Popular topics

A — Conversational framing

The label asks "What do you need help with?" rather than "Search". This makes the interaction feel like the start of a conversation, not a database query.

B — Plain English topics

The chips use client language: "Going through a divorce", not "Family Law". That helps anxious visitors recognise themselves quickly.

C — No dead ends

Unrecognised searches route to the phone number instead of a blank result. For SRB, a phone call is often the best conversion.

This sits between the hero and services grid: visible early, optional to use, and helpful for people who are not sure where to start.
