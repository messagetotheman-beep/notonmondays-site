How this works

  • Continuous improvement We don't hand over a project and leave. Work is ongoing, shaped by what matters most each week — not a fixed scope agreed once and forgotten.
  • No backlog Priorities are agreed together. Nothing sits in a queue for six months waiting for a sprint cycle. If it matters now, we work on it now.
  • Single point of accountability David owns the relationship and the work. No account managers, no handoffs, no one to translate between you and the person actually doing things.
  • Infrastructure-ready Built to extend beyond the website when the time is right — CRM, client intake, document handling, and internal systems that reflect how the firm actually works.
