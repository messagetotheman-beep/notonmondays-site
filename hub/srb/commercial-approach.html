Commercial Approach

How the foundation work and ongoing operating layer could be structured.

Last updated: 7 May 2026
← Dashboard

Overview

This sets out how the engagement could be structured commercially. The starting point is the foundation work. After that, the ongoing operating layer is how we keep the site improving rather than standing still. Wider support beyond the digital estate is available if and when it makes sense.

Part one

The Foundation

A fixed-scope project to rebuild the website, address the accessibility issues identified in the audit, clean up the technical foundations, and establish the SEO baseline. This is the work that needs to happen before anything else compounds effectively.

  • Bespoke website rebuild, designed around how SRB's clients actually navigate
  • Accessibility improvements across the site to WCAG 2.1 AA standard
  • Technical clean-up, including security, page speed, and indexing
  • SEO foundations: page structure, meta data, schema markup, and local signals
£11,500 Fixed project
Part two

The Operating Layer

Ongoing engagement to keep the site improving after the foundation is in place. This covers content updates, visibility improvements, search optimisation, and anything that needs attention each month. No backlog, no scope negotiations: priorities are agreed together and worked on continuously.

  • Continuous content updates and page improvements
  • Search and visibility work built into the monthly rhythm
  • No fixed scope: priorities are set together, updated regularly
  • Single point of accountability: David owns the work
£1,850 Per month
Optional future scope

Wider Support

Once the digital estate is working well, it becomes possible to extend into the wider operations of the firm: CRM, client intake workflows, document handling, and internal tooling. This is not part of the initial engagement. It is available if and when it makes sense for SRB.

Scoped collaboratively

If SRB moves into an ongoing operating layer retainer, £2,000 of the initial project investment is credited back.

Search and visibility is not treated as a separate add-on. It becomes part of how the site is continuously improved over time.
